# Comparison and review tools

TakeTwo — Speech analysis workspace. The CPU-only engine reuses immutable Phase 2 JSON artifacts. It does not invoke MFA, extract spectra again, read transformation windows for detection, or require new dependencies. Dataset v1 and analysis v1 remain unchanged. [Validation evidence](verification.md) distinguishes software verification from missing human evaluation.

## Contracts and storage

`src/taketwo/comparison/` contains `contracts`, `eligibility`, `reviews`, `engine`, `evaluation`, `storage` and `cli`. The executable authority is [comparison schema v1](comparison-schema-v1.json), generated by `taketwo-comparison schema`. Three independent kinds use `{sha256,result}` envelopes under `derived/comparison-v1/`: `comparison`, `review`, `evaluation`. Existing analysis readers continue to accept only their original kinds.

Comparison reports retain the full policy and its digest, comparison implementation digest, semantic dataset digest, input origins, recording/transcript/sample hashes, Phase 2 feature/alignment paths and payload hashes, and imported review references. Input summaries expose actual quality, frame counts, energy baseline, pitch-valid fraction and available rates even when comparison is withheld. Paths inside reports are managed workspace-relative paths. Publication is exclusive and content-addressed; exports never overwrite an existing file.

Observations contain canonical half-open token spans, independent native-time intervals or boundary anchors, reference and participant measurements, units, signed delta, tolerance, saturation width, normalized deviation `delta/tolerance`, excess, penalty, weight, eligibility and valid-frame fractions. Regions reference these observation IDs and deterministic explanations. Region confidence and engine timing uncertainty are explicitly null; pYIN probability is not detection confidence. Scoring, eligibility and available raw inspection evidence are distinct.

## Eligibility

Malformed units, clocks, hashes, input identities or review bounds are operational errors. Missing evidence is a scientific abstention with reason codes. Gates check recording/sample/transcript identity, verified Phase 2 text, existing pair roles/family/canonical words, source/consent/rights provenance, successful alignment, original alignment binding, independent timing and phrase reviews, feature policy compatibility, local clipping, complete-frame coverage and pitch reliability. No human approval is generated by these tools.

Each member needs two distinct independent approved timing reviews. Reviewers must cover every word and agree on a complete phrase partition. Boundary disagreements over 200 ms or rejected/unresolved decisions block comparison. Otherwise the component-wise median of checked boundaries defines correspondence; originals remain retained. This is manually assisted comparison, not an independent validation of automatic MFA accuracy. A compatible verified dataset transcript is still required: a review import alone cannot change old Phase 2 provenance.

Per dimension, `ELIGIBLE` means its gates pass, `REVIEW_REQUIRED` identifies missing review/rights evidence, `INELIGIBLE` identifies unavailable/incompatible evidence, and `PARTIALLY_ELIGIBLE` aggregates mixed regional eligibility. Overall eligibility aggregates the four dimensions. Some eligible regions can coexist with an unavailable dimension score. Pitch range-edge/octave-jump warnings or insufficient voiced coverage affect pitch alone. Activity/pause configuration incompatibility affects energy and pauses. Near-silence blocks delivery conclusions. Clipping masks contaminated spans using the saved waveform envelope, including clipping below the recording-wide warning threshold.

The CLI audits the dataset before comparison; pure engine callers must provide validated recording metadata. The full support of a feature window must lie in its reviewed word/phrase/gap. Coverage is the union of these observed supports divided by interval duration, with an 80% minimum. Pitch also needs at least 50% accepted observed frames in each member; energy statistics use active frames. The 400-sample and 1024-sample clocks are joined through native support times, never array indices or unrelated absolute positions. These conservative gates can exclude short words. No time warping, interpolation over missing frames or variance normalization hides differences.

## Measurements, detectors and scores

The four scoring measurements and all weights follow [delivery-v0](scoring-policy.md). Pacing uses matched phrase word counts and durations; pause behavior uses every shared word boundary. A gap under 150 ms is zero for the rubric. Longer gaps require complete low-energy frame corroboration; ambiguous noise/breath gaps are withheld. Relative energy/pitch excursions use NumPy linear Q90-Q10 on eligible phrase frames. Every reviewed phrase contributes expected reference-duration weight, including phrases that fail the size/coverage gates. Every inter-word boundary contributes expected weight 1. A short phrase does not disappear from the coverage denominator.

Rubric crossings produce phrase-level pacing/energy-range/pitch-range flags, extra/long pause intervals and short/missing pause boundary events. The latter have a null participant interval and a real neighboring-word anchor. There is no nonzero silence to invent when words abut. Reference locations independently use an interval or anchor. Adjacent same-dimension rubric phrase flags merge only across <=200 ms, with contiguous token spans and no intervening unflagged phrase; child observations and penalties stay separate.

Additional **evidence-only** contour detectors do not alter the four rubric scores:

- Sustained energy: corresponding word medians of normalized active energy; at least two words and 0.5 s in both members, with >=80% of words exceeding 6 dB in the same direction. The observation reports both mean word-median levels and their signed difference.
- Energy step: difference between adjacent corresponding word-median energy changes; flag absolute residual >6 dB. The marked two-word span is the resolution, not a proven abrupt frame onset.
- Pitch contour: corresponding word-median relative pitch residual, with reliable full-window evidence; flag absolute residual >3 semitones. Invalid words remain unavailable.

A fixed 1e-12 absolute epsilon handles arithmetic equality at deviation, coverage and severity boundaries; it is not measurement uncertainty. Their thresholds are versioned engineering hypotheses, not validated perceptual thresholds. They address misplaced emphasis that the original range rubric can miss, without arbitrarily redesigning it. Severity 1/2/3 bins use penalty `<=1/3`, `<=2/3`, `>2/3`; these are engineering excess bins, separate from listening-based human severity. A technical explanation prints actual values and the triggering calculation. Plain explanations locate the difference, give both measurements and a specific replay/adjustment suggestion. They do not infer emotion, intention, health or speaking quality.

Policy `delivery-v0` fixes the initial tolerances, widths, masks and equal weights. Changed tolerances require a different version; calibrated tolerances cannot fall below the initial values. `pause_calibrated_tolerance_s` represents the optional training Q95 floor in the documented max rule. Calibration itself requires real reviewed training observations; the tool does not fabricate a learned tolerance. A frozen policy requires explicit calibration/validation group IDs and an evidence reference. These are traceable assertions, not authentication of a scientific study.

## Independent review workflow

The [browser Review Workspace](review-workspace.md) now authors these same `Review` v1 records without hand-written JSON. Versioned lifecycle drafts, listening/inspection evidence and uncertainty supplement rather than replace this scientific schema; their public schemas are in [OpenAPI](api-v1.json). Final submitted exports/imports use the unchanged [comparison schema](comparison-schema-v1.json). Operator adjudication is separate history and cannot bypass the existing agreement rules; explicit same-author superseding reviews remain the reconciliation mechanism. [Actual verification](verification.md) leaves genuine human review acceptance open.

Review JSON uses the schema's `Review` contract: ID, scope (`transcript`, `timing`, `flaws`), exact Phase 2 provenance, reviewer ID/date, evidence reference, explicit independence/blinding declarations, decision (`approved`, `rejected`, `unresolved`), notes and scope-specific evidence. No checkbox defaults a human record to approved. Keep private consent and personal reviewer information outside Git.

Transcript reviews preserve the checked/corrected text separately. Two matching independent checks can be inspected with the review functions. If canonical words changed, import the corrected source and mirrors into a new dataset version with actual two-reviewer transcript metadata; regenerate alignment/features against that version. Even punctuation/original-text or verification metadata changes alter provenance. Never patch a feature artifact's verification flag.

Timing reviews require the original alignment payload digest, one checked `Interval` per canonical token (including explicit spoken-form replacements), and phrase spans partitioning all tokens. They do not modify the original `independently_reviewed=false` machine alignment. Supply previous records plus a same-reviewer `supersedes` record to correct a review; cyclic, missing and cross-subject history is rejected. Unresolved disagreements stay blocked until explicit revised reviews agree.

Flaw reviews require the participant pair ID, positive `Label` intervals or pause-event anchors, listening rationale, severity, optional uncertainty and explicitly inspected spans. Empty labels mean no positives **only within those inspected spans**. Evaluation requires full-recording coverage and two independent, prediction/recipe-blinded approved reviews. `semantic-v2` requires exact category/severity/uncertainty, like location types, interval boundary differences <=200 ms plus IoU >=0.5, or event-anchor difference <=200 ms. Reviewed-span unions must agree; event segmentation, UUIDs and independently worded rationale do not determine numeric agreement. Every pair of active reviewers must match one-to-one, so transitive chains cannot hide disagreement. Keep original IDs, explanations, disagreements and superseding records. Free-prose contradictions still require human adjudication. See [review policy](review-workspace.md). Flaw reviews are rejected as detector inputs and never change predictions.

Review artifacts record human assertions, not authenticated identities or proof of independent listening. CLI import validates binding and bounds; it does not certify the assertion. A microphone recording, actual human listening and explicit approvals remain external work.

## PowerShell usage

```powershell
uv sync --locked
uv run taketwo-comparison --help
uv run taketwo-comparison schema

# Reuse actual cached Phase 2 artifacts; no new alignment process runs.
$workspace = '.\local-data\pilot-v2'
$derived = "$workspace\derived\v1\r-478a9405c1b10a75fda31972"
$features = "$derived\features-690c8285c5e6b421f4bbdb92a977c8fcb3557b56ced3c0d6bcb15021ef36111a.json"
$alignment = "$derived\alignment-3fbaaa23e370a9d242c3b9384a8f3d080dd88ce9841efad8c934fae28ddbdc7d.json"
$report = uv run taketwo-comparison evidence $workspace --reference-features $features --reference-alignment $alignment | ConvertFrom-Json
$LASTEXITCODE # 3: scientific abstention; real mirror/reviews are missing
uv run taketwo-comparison validate $workspace --artifact $report.artifact --reproduce
uv run taketwo-comparison inspect $workspace --artifact $report.artifact
# Pick a fresh export path; existing files are protected.
uv run taketwo-comparison export $workspace --artifact $report.artifact --output .\local-data\comparison-export.json
```

The following templates need actual member artifacts, existing pair ID and independently prepared JSON files. They are not fabricated review examples; fill variables from the relevant dataset and review session.

```powershell
$review = uv run taketwo-comparison import-review $workspace --review $reviewJson --alignment $memberAlignment | ConvertFrom-Json
# Repeat import for each independent member/reviewer; preserve all returned artifact paths.
$paired = uv run taketwo-comparison compare $workspace --pair $pairId --reference-features $referenceFeatures --reference-alignment $referenceAlignment --participant-features $participantFeatures --participant-alignment $participantAlignment --review $referenceReviewA --review $referenceReviewB --review $participantReviewA --review $participantReviewB | ConvertFrom-Json
uv run taketwo-comparison detect $workspace --pair $pairId --reference-features $referenceFeatures --reference-alignment $referenceAlignment --participant-features $participantFeatures --participant-alignment $participantAlignment --review $referenceReviewA --review $referenceReviewB --review $participantReviewA --review $participantReviewB
# score accepts the same comparison inputs; evidence additionally prints observations.
uv run taketwo-comparison evaluate $workspace --report $paired.artifact --review $flawReviewA --review $flawReviewB --config $evaluationConfig
```

All comparison commands publish the same complete deterministic report for the same inputs; `detect` additionally prints regions, `evidence` prints calculations/input summaries, `score` emphasizes score availability. Exit 0 means a structurally successful command with all dimension scores available, 3 means explicit scientific abstention/incomplete evidence, 2 means operational/input failure, 130 means interruption. Inspect/export/import use exit 0 for their operation, never imply scientific eligibility. `validate --reproduce` rebuilds comparisons from saved inputs/policy without MFA or acoustic extraction. It rejects changed comparison source, dataset metadata or upstream hashes. Review/region/score JSON is the future Phase 4 service/UI authority; HTTP endpoints remain unimplemented.

## Evaluation

Evaluation accepts comparison reports and separate imported flaw reviews, never dataset recipe annotations as truth. It replays CLI report inputs and audits the dataset. Interval matching uses half-open temporal IoU, configurable default 0.5 (sensitivity runs at 0.3/0.7), maximum match cardinality then total IoU quality and ID-sorted solver ties (`matching-v2`), independently per pair/category. Each prediction and label matches once. Boundary events match only events within default 200 ms, maximum cardinality then total normalized proximity; they are reported separately from interval metrics.

Outputs include precision/recall/F1, matched median/p90 absolute boundary errors, category and severity tables, matched IDs and inspectable FP/FN IDs. Zero denominators produce null, not invented perfect accuracy. Severity-table matches require the same severity as well as the underlying category/time match; misclassified severity yields FP/FN in its bins. Spearman is null for too few/constant matched severities. Clean false flags/minute count unmatched predictions entirely within reviewed time outside all positive intervals and event neighborhoods; spans straddling positives are not counted as clean. Exact denominators and counts are retained.

`EvaluationConfig` modes are `pilot`, `held_out`, `software_fixture`. Real/synthetic origins must be separate runs. Nonfixture modes require replayable frozen connected-group splits (family, speakers, source/work/transcript, hashes, derivation and pair relationships). Evaluation groups cannot overlap calibration/validation groups; held-out mode requires `split=test`, a frozen policy and valid train/validation group provenance. No threshold is chosen from the evaluated labels. Full human held-out validation additionally requires three independent groups; fewer remains pilot-only. Missing/unresolved labels or unavailable positive-category evidence withhold aggregate metrics rather than dropping failures. Reports retain withheld-score limitations.

Family bootstrap uses 1,000 seeded resamples, percentile 95% intervals and explicit undefined-resample counts. It cannot establish generalization with a tiny corpus. `software_fixture` deliberately bypasses corpus split requirements for isolated integration checks, always disclaims independent real validation and cannot satisfy the human evaluation gate. No such bypass is used for the genuine recording.

Ground truth uses `exact-inventory-v1`. Semantic-v2 is a listening-agreement screen,
not permission to choose one person's boundaries. Every active independent approved
review must agree on the exact multiset of category, severity, uncertainty and
interval/anchor values. IDs, prose and label/list ordering are excluded from this
numerical comparison. Even tolerated boundary differences withhold all aggregate
metrics with `ground_truth_unresolved`; they can change matching, boundary errors
or clean-time denominators. This deliberately abstains more often than semantic-v2.
No labels are averaged and no operator explanation creates agreement.

Canonical evaluation labels use IDs derived from policy, pair, numerical content
and sorted occurrence index (including duplicate identical labels). All independent
review hashes, including superseded records, remain in the evaluation. Reconstruct
the canonical inventory with `resolved_flaws` from those originals. Rationale stays
in the source reviews; canonical rationale points to them. Explicit same-author
superseding reviews resolve disagreements; adjudication history remains separate.
Empty agreed inventories are valid; undefined metric denominators stay null.

Review/Evaluation v1 shapes remain compatible. The policy version is explicit in
evaluation limitations and the source implementation digest changes. Historical
artifacts remain readable and immutable; historical first-review metrics are not
current-policy results. Recompute comparisons using cached Phase 2 inputs, then
evaluate retained reviews. Never patch old artifacts or their hashes.

## Comparative insights v1

TakeTwo's comparative layer turns existing acoustic measurements into native-time
inspection targets. It produces no verified flaw, approval, delivery score,
calibrated confidence, emotion inference or independently evaluated accuracy.
`acoustic-v1`, `exploratory-v1` and `delivery-v0` remain unchanged.

### Contract and provenance

`GET /api/v1/analyses/{job_id}/insights` returns
[comparative-insights-schema-v1.json](comparative-insights-schema-v1.json).
`/insights/export` returns the complete canonical SHA-256 JSON envelope.
The original `/exploratory/export?format=json|csv` remains available and preserves
every raw candidate. New content-addressed envelopes live in
`derived/comparative-insights-v1/`; existing reports are never rewritten.

The report embeds the complete exploratory report once, with its checksum, job,
dataset version/content hash, selected members, source audio/sample/original-text
hashes, feature/alignment artifact paths and hashes, configuration and method hashes.
Common reviewed phrase partitions retain their review hashes. No feature arrays
are duplicated. Each request still validates the current managed source and
environment identities. A separate 16-entry compact cache keys the exploratory
content, comparative implementation and phrase-review hashes/partition; publication
checks existing bytes even on cache hits. Envelopes are bounded to 4 MiB.

### Evidence levels

1. **Raw feature:** actual waveform/frame measurement with native support.
2. **Automatic candidate:** a configured acoustic threshold window, not a mistake.
3. **Grouped observation:** connected compatible windows, with original IDs retained.
4. **Provisional paired segment:** corresponding supplied-text positions, not verified speech.
5. **Verified flaw region:** a separate assessment requiring independent evidence and eligibility.
6. **Independently evaluated detection:** comparison against independent held-out labels;
   software fixture tests do not establish this.

### Grouping

Reject foreign source hashes, duplicate IDs, nonfinite/incomplete measurements,
invalid native bounds and inconsistent duration. Partition by recording, exact
candidate category, energy-change sign (or low-energy family), feature hash,
method, threshold, frame window and hop resolution. Within each partition sort by
start, end and ID. Form temporal connected components when the next interval starts
at or before the component's maximum end (1e-9 s arithmetic tolerance).
Touching intervals join; positive gaps are never bridged. Transitive overlaps join.
Opposing directions, unrelated categories/configurations and recordings never join.
This is structural grouping, not an independently detected physical event.

Groups expose native union bounds/duration, original IDs, supporting window count,
median signed change, min/max changes and frame-median levels, detector configuration,
hop/window resolution, replay and limits. Overlapping raw windows are not independent
statistical support; a larger window count supplies no confidence probability.

### Word and phrase correspondence

Validate original alignment artifacts and hashes. Both must have succeeded, no OOV,
identical normalized tokens, original supplied text/hash and original token offsets.
Each must cover every canonical token index once in order with valid native bounds.
Repeated words are disambiguated by their complete canonical positions/offsets;
missing, reordered, partial, repeated ambiguous indices or mismatched text abstain.
No nearest-time, nth-output-word or fuzzy token fallback is used. Complete supplied
text mapping still does not establish the words actually spoken: omissions/repetitions
can be misaligned even in a successful MFA run. Listening remains necessary.

Each word compares native duration (`participant - reference`), relative duration
(`100 * delta / reference duration`) and preceding inter-word gap difference.
The first word has no preceding gap. Local acoustic context uses disjoint hop cells
clipped to each interval: observed support, low-energy support, frame-median energy,
linear P90-P10 energy range and energy relative to that recording's baseline.
No missing frame/tail is called quiet. Cross-take relative-energy delta is withheld
unless both intervals have complete support and valid baselines. Digital amplitude
does not establish microphone-independent effort or perceived loudness.

Phrase comparison requires equal complete phrase partitions from validated
independent approved timing consensus for each selected source and original alignment.
The API hashes those reviews into the cache/result. Phrase intervals use the first
and last provisional machine word bounds within that explicit partition, so they
remain labelled unverified. Without a common reviewed partition, phrase comparison
is withheld; punctuation, pauses and matching wall-clock times do not invent phrases.

### Alignment-gap diagnostics

Flag a gap between consecutive indexed machine words when it is at least 1 s.
Where paired correspondence exists, it must also exceed the other take's gap by
at least 0.5 s. These are inspection thresholds, not validated perceptual cutoffs.
Include actual before/after words, gap/context bounds, replay, independent hop-cell
activity measurements, selection reason and unavailable alignment confidence.
Never convert an alignment gap into a pause candidate or flaw.

The genuine reference/uneven pair has a 2.890 s machine gap after `technology`
(10.12–10.50 s) before `is` (13.39–13.47 s). The reference has a zero gap there.
Complete acoustic support in the participant gap has **0.000 s low-energy support**
under acoustic-v1's activity rule. This does not support a quiet-pause conclusion.
Misalignment, text mismatch, repeated/omitted speech or another cause requires listening.
Confidence and independently checked timing accuracy remain unavailable.

### Inspection order

No priority score is produced. First place evidence with complete native support,
RMS >=0.001, no clipping and no stored recording-quality flags; limited evidence
follows. This is a diagnostic screen, not proof of clean speech: no isolated noise
reference/calibrated SNR exists. Categories then sort: alignment anomaly, preceding
gap difference (absolute delta >=0.15 s), word duration difference (>=0.1 s), grouped
acoustic observation. Within category sort by descending measured gap/duration delta
or group union duration, then native start and evidence ID. Amplitude alone cannot
outrank other evidence. Every item includes its reason; no confidence or severity.

The following-word context of an alignment anomaly is not highlighted again as a
gap/duration item. Other words with both gap and duration changes use one gap item;
all original word measurements remain in the complete appendix/JSON. Main view starts
with six inspection items; the full ordered inventory and all groups remain accessible.

Genuine initial output: **53 raw windows → 44 groups**, 71 provisional word segments,
one gap anomaly. Grouping alone does not make all 44 observations important; the
initial selection and full inventory are distinct. Recording-level −0.854375 s
(−2.826775%) and +0.043847 dB RMS differences establish no flaw or perceptual significance.
Phrase comparison is withheld because independent phrase partitions are absent.

### Listening, reporting and limitations

Each A/B action reuses one existing native audio element and its own bounds. Switching
pauses the other track; audio is neither stretched nor synchronized by equal seconds.
Inspect moves keyboard focus to the selected native audio control. Return-to-complete
clears bounded selection and restores its full window. Navigation pauses playback.
Exploratory listening never submits reviewer completion or independent approval.

**Analysis Summary** prints the overview, first three selected items, concrete
selection reasons, listening questions, independent native intervals, limits and
verification status. **Full Evidence Report** adds clearly marked appendices that
preserve all groups, paired measurements with stable IDs, all anomalies, original
candidates, provisional word lists, methods and source hashes. JSON/CSV provide
full precision and are not truncated. Reference-only reports use single-recording
inspection terminology. The separate
six-page hackathon technical draft and historical PDFs remain separate.

The v1 contract remains unchanged. `order_reason` now explains the actual supporting
measurement and inspection threshold before the precise deterministic order policy.
The primary view shows that item-specific reason; expanded evidence retains the whole
policy. No detector, grouping threshold, identity or score semantics changed. New
comparative implementation hashes retain historical envelopes intact. Grouping audit
found no compatible overlapping/touching leftovers in the genuine 44-group inventory;
remaining connected overlaps have opposing directions and remain separate.

[Human listening acceptance](review-protocol.md#product-listening-acceptance) evaluates product
usefulness, separately from independent annotations and detection accuracy. Actual
listening remains pending until a person records their review.

Low pitch coverage and the existing independent F0 limitation remain explicit; no
new validation data justifies a changed extractor, confidence mask or scoring gate.
Actual Narrator/NVDA acceptance, independent timing/F0/flaw truth, rights and calibration
remain human/external dependencies. Codex assisted code, tests and documentation under
