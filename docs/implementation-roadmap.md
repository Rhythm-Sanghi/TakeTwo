## Evidence gates

Prioritize data and temporal evidence before interface polish. Genuine masters or successful alignment are not a complete contrastive dataset. A complete automatic word list can still fit an incorrect transcript. Obtain independent word-start/end labels, retain failures, and publish median/p90 absolute boundary error and counts only after actual evaluation. Initial engineering targets remain median <=100 ms and p90 <=250 ms; they are not official criteria or measured results.

Target application peak memory <6 GB and a pair of 60-second excerpts within five minutes remains a future target. Phase 2 currently limits features to 30 seconds, executes serially and reports measured short-clip costs; do not extrapolate its measurements to the larger target. Failed/timeout cases remain evidence. Same-environment feature replay is tested; cross-platform tolerance and alignment repeatability must be measured separately.

Before held-out scoring evaluation, freeze eligibility, masks, tolerances and rubric using reviewed training/validation examples. Proposed later utility targets remain precision >=0.75, recall >=0.60 at IoU 0.5 and <=1 false flag/min on human-reviewed clean spans; no such result exists. Fewer than three independent test groups permits pilot-only findings. Never tune the sealed test to pass a target.
